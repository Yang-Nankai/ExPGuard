Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How does per-site VPN protection work?

Per-site VPN protection routes traffic from specific websites through our secure VPN servers. Add a site to your protected list, and all requests to that domain will be encrypted and routed through our network.

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

Free Plan: Unlimited per-site VPN protection for any website you choose.

Premium Plan: Adds global VPN for all traffic, 70+ countries, faster speeds, and priority support.

Is my browsing data logged?

We have a strict no-logs policy. We don't track, collect, or store your browsing activity, connection logs, or IP addresses. Your privacy is our top priority.

Can I use this on multiple devices?

Yes! Install the extension on any browser that supports Chrome extensions. Your account syncs across all devices.
