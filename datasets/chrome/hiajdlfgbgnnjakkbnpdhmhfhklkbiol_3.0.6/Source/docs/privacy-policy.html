Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2025

Short version: JustBlock Security does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. All filtering happens on-device using Chrome’s Manifest V3 declarativeNetRequest API.

What data we collect

We do not collect or transmit personally identifiable information. The extension stores small settings locally (e.g., which rule packs you enabled, whitelist entries) using Chrome’s storage API. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions we use

  • declarativeNetRequest: to block requests based on static rulesets you enable.
  • storage: to save your preferences (enabled packs, master toggle, whitelist).

Browsing activity

We do not read, log, or transmit your browsing history. Blocking uses declarative rules executed by the browser.

Contact

If you have questions, contact us at support@justblock.org.
