TITLE
JustBlock – Popup Blocker & Phishing Protection

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Block popups, phishing & unsafe sites in real-time. Privacy-first protection with visible live activity.

PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION
JustBlock requires website access to provide real-time protection on pages the user visits. This access is used locally to detect and block automatic popups, phishing pages, suspicious redirects and tracking parameters. JustBlock does not collect, store, sell or transmit browsing data.
