🛒 What Is Share-A-Cart?

Share-A-Cart is a free browser extension that lets you share your online shopping cart with anyone. Whether you're planning a grocery trip, sending a gift list, or managing a classroom supply order, Share-A-Cart makes collaborative shopping simple and efficient.


🚀 How to Share a Shopping Cart

1. Shop online like usual at your favorite retailer.
2. Use Share-A-Cart to generate a unique cart sharing link.
3. Send the link to anyone - they can instantly view and load your cart on their device.

❓FAQs

Q: Where is the extension icon?
A: It's on the right side of your toolbar. You may want to pin it so that it's always there. Just click the icon that looks like a puzzle piece, and then click the pin icon next to Share-A-Cart.

Q: What does Share-A-Cart do with my user data?
A: We don't sell user data to any data brokers. You can access most of our features for free and without giving us your email address or going through any registration process.

Q: If I send a link to a cart to someone, do they need the extension as well?
A: Not always. Many major retailers are fully supported without requiring the extension on the recipient's end.

Q: What if I find a retailer that you don't support? Or if I have an issue?
A: Email us at info@share-a-cart.com, and we'll get back to you right away. We are adding support for new retailers every week.

Q: Can the person I send a cart link to edit my cart?
A: Yes! The receiver of the cart can add or remove items and change quantities. This is really helpful if you are sending a giant wishlist (like a Christmas gift list) to someone because it allows them to choose exactly what they want to order.

Q: Why should I use Share-A-Cart instead of other extensions that do the same thing?
A: No other extension supports as many stores as Share-A-Cart, and we have many helpful features that our competitors don't. We have been in business since 2018, and this is not a 'side project' for us. And if you report an idea for a feature, new retailer, or a bug, we get back to you right away!

NOTE: Share-A-Cart does not complete any purchases on your behalf. It only helps shoppers move items from their cart to a recipient's cart. All checkouts must still be completed by the user. Share-A-Cart is not related to or endorsed by any of our listed retailers.
