OrganizEat Extension

What it does

This Extension to your browser allows you to easily sent to import any recipes you currently browsed to.

How it works

The OrganizEat extension becomes kind of part of your browser. Click on it, you see a button that allows you to start the import process. Why yet another click? Just to prevent you from accidentally importing a random recipe. Your recipes' collection is well organized and we go extra mile to keep it that way.
